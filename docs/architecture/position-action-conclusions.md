# Frozen position action conclusions

The `position-action.1.0.0` case contract composes a committed security assessment
with committed concentration, stress, liquidity and drawdown reports. The host
saves a `POSITION_ACTION` stage and a `position_action` report projection through
the existing decision ledger. The framework cannot supply this projection.

`PositionActionCommand` binds the security and lifecycle, knowledge cutoff,
portfolio authorization, exact report event identities, execution routes and
synthetic policy version. Its qualification scope binds the owner, complete
account set, source, market state, board, initial holding domain and selected
downside grid. A qualification evidence digest binds the complete policy and
calendar. Caller-supplied established targets are rejected; the host derives
targets from committed reports and saved conclusions.

The conclusions are directional advice with quantity ceilings. They create no
orders or fills and never authorize a purchase. A hypothetical execution plan
does not restore personal risk or discharge an obligation.

## Decision composition

An independently verified legal termination or preregistered thesis falsification
retains an exit direction and a zero target through missing statistical, cost or
position evidence. A missing quantity remains unknown. Independently committed
personal risk targets also survive an unavailable security assessment.

Without a security hard gate, the host mechanically selects the policy's normal
or risk downside grid using the applicable issuer concentration and portfolio
stress, liquidity and drawdown states. An unavailable selected qualification
does not cause a switch to another grid.

The statistical matrix uses directional guarded bounds. Reliable value denial
can exit independently of a downside guard. Risky downside with value not denied
can reduce. Holding requires both safe downside and established value. Complete
intermediate combinations abstain. Policy threshold bands must be disjoint.

Holding uses the reconciled current quantity. The first qualified statistical
reduction freezes an aggregate quantity anchor across the authorized accounts.
Later assessments retain that anchor and the strictest saved target through
partial sales or repurchases. The first qualified statistical exit program also
freezes a market-value cost anchor. Prices and subsequent quantities do not
replace its cost denominator.

A quantity-changing corporate action blocks reuse of an unadjusted reduction
anchor. The host retains the frozen facts and reports the unresolved quantity
basis; it does not infer an adjustment ratio from a ledger delta.

The host conjoins statistical, concentration, stress, liquidity, drawdown and
exit targets through the existing execution planner. Routing restrictions keep
the direction, target and unfilled quantity visible. A legally rounded full sale
retains the reduction decision and requires confirmation. No route is an order.

## Costs and replay

Primary account cost evidence must cover the current cutoff and fit the qualified
policy envelope before statistics can authorize an action. Paid fee entries after
the first exit-program anchor and the current disposal plan share its cumulative
cost budget. Statistical cost failures preserve the model direction for diagnosis
and retain independently established targets. Missing cost evidence is reported
as a data observation; costs outside the envelope cause statistical abstention.

The execution planner may use its separately supported conservative cost bounds
for deterministic protection. Such a fallback does not qualify statistical use.

The frozen case, conclusion, anchors and execution projection are saved before
publication. Replaying the same business identity returns the saved report and
does not recompute or create another reduction anchor. CLI replay and authenticated
scoped report reads use that same immutable projection. Existing historical
reports omit the new optional field when it is absent.

All examples and qualification fixtures in this contract are independent D0
synthetic software fixtures. They do not provide real statistical qualification,
personal activation, live evidence, deployment or a release authorization.
