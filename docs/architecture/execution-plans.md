# Frozen execution plans

The `execution.1.0.0` case, host and report contracts compose committed
concentration, portfolio-stress, liquidity and capital-protection outcomes.
The frozen command names their event identities, the same complete account
scope, portfolio authorization and position cutoff. Qualified pre-existing
quantity ceilings are explicit inputs. This command does not generate
statistical signals or certify their qualification.

The host retains the lowest applicable quantity ceiling and its obligation
identities. A zero ceiling remains an exit target. Missing evidence blocks
allocation without silently discarding independently established obligations.
Backdated or incomplete-scope requests cannot advance target history. A
quantity-changing corporate action after an earlier target requires a resolved
quantity basis; this version blocks instead of guessing an adjustment factor.
Missing securities likewise require reconciliation, not assumed closure.

Existing security targets receive legal allocations first. Remaining portfolio
shortfalls use proportional reductions of the remaining account-local sellable
exposure. Each candidate is legally rounded and its hypothetical stress,
cash and exposure gaps are recomputed. Dated cash obligations reuse the
liquidity module's account-and-time transfer network. Late proceeds cannot
cover an earlier deadline, and an unusable cash route does not justify
selling additional quantities when it cannot improve the remaining gaps.

Account routes carry frozen rule evidence, legal quantity increments, sellable
and transferable times, and full disposal-cost curves. Minimal legal quantities
take precedence over cost. Among those allocations, account funding obligations
and the first sellable window precede verified cost comparisons. Minimum
commissions and dividend-lot charges are included. An evidenced conservative
bound enables proportional fallback for that security only; absent both verified
costs and a bound, allocation remains blocked. Exhaustive account alternatives
have explicit safety limits; exceeding them reports `EXECUTION_SEARCH_LIMIT`,
not a proof of economic infeasibility.

A reduction that legally rounds to a full sale keeps its positive ceiling and
reduction direction, with an explicit reconfirmation requirement. Later
portfolio allocation does not erase that distinction. All sale legs are
unconfirmed hypothetical plans. Neither a plan nor user acknowledgement
constitutes a fill, risk restoration, an order or permission to add exposure.

The existing authenticated report API and read-only web projection expose
committed results. There is no new mutation endpoint, order path, live adapter,
personal policy default, activation or deployment. Tests use independently
constructed synthetic cases through the real frozen-case runner and ledger.
